Hi Kevin,

I'm sorry to hear about your grandmother's passing.

Per our Bereavement and Family Leave policy, for the death of a grandparent you're entitled to up to 3 paid days off. These days don't need to be taken consecutively, but must be used within 60 days, and this leave doesn't reduce your PTO or sick balance. No documentation is required — just let your supervisor (Scott Bailey) know as soon as you can so the time off can be arranged.

(Source: bereavement-and-family-leave.html)

Wishing you and your family comfort during this time.
