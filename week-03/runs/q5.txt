Funds Availability Policy > Longer holds
Source file: funds-availability-policy.html
About this document: Version 3 (current). Owner: Deposit Operations.
Context from elsewhere in this document: Uses the term "business days," which is defined in Section 2: "A business day is Monday through Friday, except federal holidays. A deposit made before 5 p.m. on a business day is received that day. A deposit made after 5 p.m., or on a weekend or holiday, is received on the next business day."

Larkfield may hold check deposits for up to seven business days when the day's check deposits total more than $6,000, when the account has been open fewer than 30 days, when the account has been overdrawn repeatedly in the last six months, or when there is reason to doubt the check will be paid. The member is told about a longer hold when the deposit is made, or by mail if the hold is placed later.

A deposit made before 5 p.m. on a business day is received that day. A deposit made after 5 p.m., or on a weekend or holiday, is received on the next business day."

Cash deposited at a branch, direct deposits such as payroll, and incoming wire transfers are available on the business day they are received.

ANSWER
Based on the policy, for checks deposited at a branch, the first $275 of the day's check deposits is available on the next business day, with the remainder available on the second business day after the deposit [funds-availability-policy.html]. So for a $2,000 paycheck, the member can use **$275** on the next business day, with the remaining $1,725 available on the second business day after the deposit [funds-availability-policy.html].

Note: this assumes no longer hold applies — longer holds (up to seven business days) can be placed in certain circumstances, such as if the account has been open fewer than 30 days, has been repeatedly overdrawn in the last six months, or if there is reason to doubt the check will be paid [funds-availability-policy.html]. The sources don't indicate whether any of those conditions apply to this member.
